Attendance & daily diary

The school day, recorded as it happens

Registers, diary entries and homework go in from the classroom on a phone or the web portal, and parents see them the same day, without a single paper slip going home.

  • Mark a class in a minutePresent, absent, late or on leave: one tap per student, with the whole class pre-filled as present.
  • Works without signalThe Android app saves the register offline and syncs it the moment the phone is back online.
  • Daily diary and homeworkTeachers post the day's diary and homework per class and subject, with due dates parents can see.
  • Staff attendance tooTeachers' own check-ins sit alongside the student registers, ready for payroll at month end.

Exams, results & report cards

From marks entry to a printed report card

Teachers enter marks once. Grades, positions and skills are worked out for them, coordinators approve, and the report card is ready to print or share with parents.

  • Exams on a scheduleSet terms, papers and total marks per class and subject, and draw questions from the school's question bank.
  • Marks entry, then approvalTeachers enter marks; work from teachers on probation goes to the coordinator or principal to approve.
  • Results worked out for youPercentages, grades and class positions are calculated the moment marks are saved, so no spreadsheets.
  • Report cards worth printingPrintable report cards with skills and remarks, in your school's branding, plus certificates when needed.

Fees, challans & payroll

Every rupee in and out, accounted for

Fee structures, family challans, payment proofs and receipts on one side; payroll and expenses on the other. The accountant sees the whole picture, and the principal sees the summary.

  • Fee structures per classTuition, admission, transport and other heads set once per class and session, with sibling discounts applied.
  • Family challansOne challan for all the children in a family, printed or shared with parents, with due dates and late fines.
  • Payment proofs verifiedParents upload a bank or wallet receipt; the accountant verifies it and the receipt is issued in Rs.
  • Payroll and expensesSalaries from staff attendance, school expenses and accounts in one place, reported per branch.

Parent portal

Parents stay close without calling the office

One sign-in for every child in the family. Attendance, homework, results, fee challans and messages to teachers, on the web or the Android app.

  • Every child, one sign-inA child switcher moves between brothers and sisters, even across branches of the same school.
  • Sign in their wayAdmission number and password, or a one-time code sent to their email, with two-step verification if they want it.
  • The whole school dayAttendance, diary, homework, timetable and results for each child, updated as teachers save them.
  • Fees and messagesSee and pay challans, upload payment proof, and message the class teacher directly.

Security & control

Student records you can stand behind

Every account is created by the school, every action is checked against a role, and every change leaves a trail. Parents trust you with their children's details, and the system is built to keep that trust.

Permissions, deny by default

Every role starts with nothing and is granted only what it needs. A teacher sees their own classes; an accountant sees fees, not marks. Checks run on the server, not just on the screen.

Only school accounts sign in

There is no public sign-up. Every account is created by the school, and two-step verification with an authenticator app protects the accounts that matter most.

Encrypted backups, tested restores

Backups are encrypted, and each one is checked by restoring it, so the backup you are counting on is one that actually works.

Full audit history

Who changed a mark, a fee or a student record, and when, is kept on file. History is never quietly rewritten.

Auto sign-out when idle

A session left open on a shared office computer signs itself out after a period of inactivity, so the next person cannot carry on as you.

Private by design
